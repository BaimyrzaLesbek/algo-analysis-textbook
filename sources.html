Sources

Books and course materials

  1. Levitin A. Introduction to the Design and Analysis of Algorithms, 3rd ed. Pearson, 2012. Sections 2.1–2.5, Appendices A (useful formulas) and B (recurrence relations). Main source: the plans of analysis, pseudocode notation and the examples MaxElement, UniqueElements, MatrixMultiplication, Binary, SequentialSearch, Factorial, Tower of Hanoi, BinRec and Fibonacci.
  2. Cormen T. H., Leiserson C. E., Rivest R. L., Stein C. Introduction to Algorithms, 4th ed. MIT Press, 2022. Chapters 3, 4, Appendix A — asymptotic notation, recurrences, summations.
  3. Sedgewick R., Wayne K. Algorithms, 4th ed. Addison-Wesley, 2011. Section 1.4 — empirical analysis and operation counting (idea of the empirical tasks).
  4. Kleinberg J., Tardos É. Algorithm Design. Pearson, 2005. Chapters 2, 5 — asymptotic analysis, divide-and-conquer recurrences.
  5. Graham R. L., Knuth D. E., Patashnik O. Concrete Mathematics, 2nd ed. Addison-Wesley, 1994. Chapters 1, 2, 7 — recurrences (Tower of Hanoi), sums, generating functions.
  6. Lecture 4. Mathematical Analysis of Nonrecursive Algorithms (course materials).
  7. Lecture 5. Mathematical Analysis of Recursive Algorithms (course materials).

Tools

  • MathJax 3 — rendering of mathematical formulas.
  • Python 3 — code for the empirical tasks (code/ folder); matplotlib for optional plots.

Original content

The worked examples marked Original are not in the lectures: SelectionSort (the algorithm itself appears in Levitin §3.1; the step-by-step presentation here is our own), PrefixAverages, RecMax and Power (fast exponentiation). All quiz questions and problems were written by the authors and are not copied from the lectures or from Levitin's exercises, including the halving-loop problem, the skewed average-case search, the Stars recursion, the climbing-stairs problem and the "find the mistake" problems. Every closed-form answer was checked by the program code/verify_answers.py.
